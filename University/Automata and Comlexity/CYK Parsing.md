- [[Compiler Construction|Bottom-up parsing]] applies rules backwards, it tries to construct the starting variable $S$ from the input word
- **CYK algorithm**: bottom-up parsing technique for [[Chomsky Normal Form]]
## Algorithm
Let $G$ be a grammar in [[Chomsky Normal Form]].
- **Goal**: determine whether word $w\neq \lambda$ is in $L(G)$
- **Idea**: compute sets $V_{u}$ of variables ($u$ subword of $w$) such that $$V_{u}=\{A\in V \mid A \Rightarrow^{+}u\}$$ as follows: 
	- if $|u|=1$, then $V_{u}=\{A\in V \mid A\to u \in P\}$
	- if $|u| > 1$, then $V_{u}$ is the set of all $A \in V$ such that
		- $u=u_{1}u_{2}$ for some non-empty words $u_{1}$, $u_{2}$ and
		- $A\to BC \in P$ with $B\in V_{u_{1}}$ and $C \in V_{u_{2}}$
	- Finally, $w\in L(G) \Leftrightarrow S\in V_{w}$

> [!question] 
> Use CYK to check whether $abbb$ is generated by $$S\to AB \qquad A\to BB \mid a \qquad B\to AB \mid b$$
> > [!info]- Solution
> > We have 
> > $$\begin{align}
> > V_{a} &= \{A\} \text{ since } A\to a \\
> > V_{b} &= \{B\} \text{ since } B\to b \\
> > V_{ab} &= \{X \, \vert\, X \to V_{a}V_{b} = \{AB\}\} = \{S, B\} \\
> > V_{bb} &= \{X \, \vert\, X \to V_{b}V_{b} = \{BB\}\} = \{A\} \\
> > V_{abb} &= \{X \, \vert\, X \to V_{a}V_{bb} \cup V_{ab}V_{b} = \{AA, SB, BB\}\} = \{A\} \\
> > V_{bbb} &= \{X \, \vert\, X \to V_{b}V_{bb} \cup V_{bb}V_{b} = \{BA, AB\}\} = \{S,B\} \\
> > V_{abbb} &= \{X \, \vert\, X \to V_{a}V_{bbb} \cup V_{ab}V_{bb}\cup V_{abbb}V_{b}\} \\
> > &= \{X \, \vert\, X \to \{AS,AB,SA,BA\}\} = \{S,B\} \\
> > \end{align}$$
> > The word $abbb$ is in the language since $S\in V_{abbb}$: 
> > $$\underbrace{S}_{abbb} \to \underbrace{A}_{a} \underbrace{B}_{bbb} \to \underbrace{A}_{a} \underbrace{A}_{bb} \underbrace{B}_{b} \to \underbrace{A}_{a} \underbrace{B}_{b} \underbrace{B}_{b} \underbrace{B}_{b} \to^{4} abbb$$
