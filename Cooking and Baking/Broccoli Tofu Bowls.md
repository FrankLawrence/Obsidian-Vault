Veggies:
- [ ] 1 very large or 2 smaller sweet potatoes (500-600g)
- [ ] 1 broccoli
- [ ] 1 tsp salt, pepper, sweet paprika, smoked paprika, kurkuma
- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil

Tofu:
- [ ] 2 cubes tofu (can be smoked)
- [ ] 1 tsp smoked paprika
- [ ] 2 tbsp soy sauce
- [ ] 2 tbsp sriracha (substitute with 1 tsp spicy paprika and 1 tbsp maple syrup)
- [ ] 1 tbsp olive oil

Sun-dried tomato sauce
- [ ] 1 glass oatmilk (or 1/2 glass soy cream and 1/2 glass water) (or 1 glass water and 1 tbsp peanut butter)
- [ ] 1/2 jar sun-dried tomatoes
- [ ] 1-2 garlic cloves
- [ ] 1/2 tsp salt, pepper, sweet paprika, oregano, basil, kurkuma
- [ ] if needs thickening: cornstarch
- [ ] to serve: fresh parsley/basil/schnittlauch/sesame

Steps:
1. Heat the oven to 180°C (356F). Dice the vegetables coarsely and throw them onto a tray. Cover with olive oil and the spices. Bake for 35 minutes. Add 5 minutes more if taking them straight from the fridge.
2. Dice the tofu more finely and put in a bowl. Add the spices and sauce and mix. Throw onto another tray and cover with olive oil. Bake for 30 minutes.
3. Make the sauce. Blend the ingredients. Heat in a pan with cornstarch if it needs thickening.
4. Serve in bowls with sesame/other.
