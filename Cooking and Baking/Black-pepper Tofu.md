Ingredients:
Crispy Baked Tofu:
- [ ] 1 block tofu
- [ ] 1 tbsp cornstarch
- [ ] salt, pepper, oil
- [ ] rice

Black Pepper Sauce:
- [ ] 8 tbsp soy sauce (mix 5 light - 3 dark)
- [ ] 1 tbsp+ black pepper
- [ ] 4 tbsp maple syrup
- [ ] 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- [ ] 2 tbsp water
- [ ] 1 tbsp cornstarch

Veggies:
- [ ] 5 shallots/red onions (generously)
- [ ] bell peppers (large one)
- [ ] mild chillies
- [ ] 5 cloves garlic
- [ ] 2 tsp ginger (good chunk)

Steps:
1. Dice the tofu into large cubes. Coat it in cornstarch, shake off the excess. Heat some oil in a wok. Fry the tofu in a few small batches until they are golden brown. Set aside on paper towels.
2. Make your rice now if you haven't already.
3. Clean the pan, then add the onions, bell peppers, and chillies. Saute on medium heat for about 5 minutes until they all brighten.
4. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for one more minute, stirring constantly.
5. Add the tofu back in together with the sauce. Stir to combine, then cook another minute.
6. Serve over cooked rice.
