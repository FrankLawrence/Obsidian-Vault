## Roadmap Requirements
### Elicitation
> [!summary] Determine requirements with stakeholders

- Requirement sources include: 
	- Stake-holders
	- High-level system objectives
	- Domain knowledge
	- Operational context
	- Organizational context
### Analysis
> [!summary] Are requirements clear, consistent, complete?

- usually vague
- models help with making requirements more precise
- validation and analysis sometimes merged
### Specification
> [!summary] Document desired system behavior

- Question of **what** and not *how*
- usage of natural language (ambiguous) and system models

### Validation
> [!summary] Are the requirements what the stake-holders want

- We use different techniques to check this:
	- Prototypes or flip-chart mockups
	- Use informal or formal models
