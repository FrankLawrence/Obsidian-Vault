### **Positive Learning Environment**

We all want to maintain a positive learning environment! In particular, as a sign of respect towards your fellow students, teaching assistants, and the lecturer, please:

- **Be silent while the lecturer is explaining.** You can always raise your hand to ask questions.
- **Arrive on time** for lectures and tutorials.
- **Use appropriate language and tone** in discussions.

### **Prerequisites**

The first part of the course (Discrete Mathematics) builds upon some of the concepts introduced in the "Logic and Sets" course. In particular, familiarity with *proof by induction* is particularly important, as it will play a key role in solving problems and understanding the material in this part.  

The second part of the course (Calculus) builds upon the foundational mathematics that is typically taught in high school programs around the world. As such, some prerequisites will be assumed, most of which can be found in the Preliminaries chapter of the Calculus textbook.

### **Grading**

The written **final exam** **determines the final grade for 100%**.

The resit exam is also written and (re-)determines the final grade for 100%.

Additionally, you can earn a **bonus of 0.5 points** added to your final grade as follows:

- Each week, you will participate in two tutorials and complete a set of exercises as homework (available on Canvas).
- You are encouraged to write your solutions **on paper** and submit them to your tutor at the **end of the final tutorial of the week.**
- **Exception:**  April 23 and May 7 count as one week, with submissions due on May 7.
- Collaboration with peers is allowed, but each student must write their solutions independently.
- **Not all exercises must be submitted.** Some weeks contain a large number of exercises; while you are encouraged to attempt as many as possible, you are not required to submit every single one. The key is to show substantial effort in your submission.
- Since the course runs for six weeks, you will have six opportunities to submit your exercises. Tutors will mark the submissions as either "pass" (indicating that the exercises were submitted with significant effort) or "not pass" (indicating either no submission or insufficient effort).
- If you receive a "pass" for at least five out of six assignments, you will receive a bonus of 0.5 points added to your final grade.

**Note:** If you earn the 0.5-point bonus, it will be automatically included in the grade visible on Canvas for both the final exam and the resit exam.
