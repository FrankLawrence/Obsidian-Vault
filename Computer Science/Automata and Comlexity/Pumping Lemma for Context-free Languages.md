> [!info] THEOREM
> Let $L$ be a context-free language. There exists $m> 0$ such that for every word $w\in L$ with $|w| \geq m$: $$w=uvxyz$$ with $|vxy| \leq m$ and $|vy|\geq 1$, and $uv^{i}xy^{i}z\in L$ for every $i\geq 0$.
